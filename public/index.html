How is uptime calculated?

Monitored services (websites, servers): uptime comes from HetrixTools, which checks each one around the clock from its monitoring locations. A day's percentage is the share of those checks that succeeded.

Everything else (no automatic monitor): uptime comes from our posted incidents. Time under a partial or major outage counts as down; degraded performance and maintenance don't.

Each bar is one day, from midnight to midnight UTC. Uptime only counts from the day monitoring began.
